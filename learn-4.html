Stock Market Introduction

Understand how stocks work before putting a single rupee in.

What is a stock?

When you buy a stock, you buy a small ownership stake in a company. If the company grows and becomes more valuable, your stake is worth more. If it struggles, your stake loses value. Stocks are one of the best long-term wealth-building tools — but they require patience.

How the stock market works

Companies list on stock exchanges (NSE, BSE in India) to raise money from the public. Investors buy and sell these shares on the exchange through a broker. The price is simply what someone is willing to pay right now — driven by earnings, sentiment, news, and future expectations.

Key concepts

  • Bull market — prices rising, economy doing well, investors optimistic
  • Bear market — prices falling 20%+ from recent highs
  • Dividend — a share of the company's profit paid to shareholders
  • P/E ratio — Price ÷ Earnings. A rough gauge of how expensive a stock is relative to its earnings
  • Market cap — total value of a company's shares outstanding (Large-cap: safe, Mid/Small-cap: riskier but higher potential)
  • Portfolio — the collection of all your investments

How to start investing in stocks

  1. Open a demat + trading account with Zerodha, Upstox, Angel One, etc.
  2. Complete KYC (PAN + Aadhaar)
  3. Start with index funds or large-cap stocks — not penny stocks
  4. Invest money you won't need for at least 5 years
  5. Never invest borrowed money

Principles of good stock investing

  • Diversify — don't put all money in one stock or one sector
  • Buy quality — invest in companies with strong fundamentals, not hype
  • Think long-term — 10+ year horizon smooths out volatility
  • Ignore the noise — news, tips, and predictions are mostly noise
  • Don't try to time the market — time IN the market beats timing the market
⚠️ Warning: Intraday trading and F&O (Futures & Options) are not investing — they're speculation. Studies show 90%+ of retail traders lose money in F&O. Avoid until you have years of experience.

Taxes on stock gains (India)

  • STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains, held <1 year) — taxed at 20%
  • LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains, held >1 year) — taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh
