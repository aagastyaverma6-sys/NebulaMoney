Credit Score & Loans

Your credit score is your financial reputation. Here's how to build and protect it.

What is a credit score?

A credit score (CIBIL score in India) is a 3-digit number between 300 and 900 that represents your creditworthiness. Lenders use it to decide whether to give you a loan and at what interest rate. Higher score = better terms.

750+
Excellent — best loan rates available
650–749
Good — most loans approved
<650
Poor — loan rejection or high interest

What affects your CIBIL score?

  • Payment history (35%) — paying bills and EMIs on time is the biggest factor
  • Credit utilization (30%) — how much of your credit limit you use. Keep it below 30%
  • Length of credit history (15%) — older accounts help your score
  • Credit mix (10%) — having both secured (home loan) and unsecured (credit card) credit
  • New credit inquiries (10%) — applying for too many loans/cards hurts your score

How to improve your credit score

  1. Pay all EMIs and credit card bills on time — set auto-pay
  2. Keep credit card utilization below 30% of your limit
  3. Don't close old credit card accounts — they help credit history length
  4. Avoid applying for multiple loans/cards in a short period
  5. Check your CIBIL report for errors at least once a year (free at cibil.com)

Understanding Loans

A loan is borrowed money you must repay with interest over a fixed period. The key factors in any loan are:

  • Principal — amount borrowed
  • Interest rate — annual cost of borrowing (ROI)
  • Tenure — repayment period in months
  • EMI — Equated Monthly Installment (principal + interest, fixed monthly payment)
  • Processing fee — one-time fee charged by lender (0.5–2%)
📊 EMI Formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1) — where P = principal, r = monthly rate, n = tenure in months. Use our EMI Calculator instead!

Good debt vs Bad debt

  • Good debt — home loan (asset that appreciates), education loan (increases earning power), business loan (generates income)
  • Bad debt — credit card revolving balance (24–45% interest!), personal loans for consumption, buy-now-pay-later for lifestyle

Managing debt smartly

  • Avalanche method: pay off highest-interest debt first (saves most money)
  • Snowball method: pay off smallest balance first (builds momentum)
  • Never miss an EMI — even one missed payment damages your score significantly
  • Prepay when possible — reduces total interest paid
