Crypto & Alt Investments

Beyond stocks and FDs — understanding alternative asset classes.

What are alternative investments?

Alternative investments are assets outside traditional stocks, bonds, and cash. They include cryptocurrency, gold, REITs, P2P lending, startups, and collectibles. They can add diversification and potentially higher returns — but usually come with higher risk, lower liquidity, and less regulation.

Cryptocurrency

Digital currencies secured by cryptography on a blockchain network. Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) are the largest by market cap.

  • High volatility: 50–80% drawdowns are common — not for the faint-hearted
  • Tax in India: 30% flat tax on crypto gains + 1% TDS on every transaction above ₹50,000
  • No loss offset: Crypto losses cannot be offset against other income
  • Use case: Speculative allocation — max 5–10% of portfolio, only money you can afford to lose
⚠️ Warning: Altcoins, meme coins, and "guaranteed return" crypto schemes are extremely high-risk. Many are outright scams. If someone promises guaranteed returns in crypto, it's fraud.

Gold

The original alternative asset. Gold holds its value over centuries and acts as a hedge against inflation, currency devaluation, and geopolitical crises.

  • Physical gold: Jewellery (high making charges), coins, bars
  • Digital gold: Buy grams online via Paytm, PhonePe — stored in vaults
  • Gold ETF: Trade on stock exchange like shares — best for most investors
  • Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB): Government-issued, earns 2.5% interest + gold price appreciation. Most recommended form.
  • Allocation: 5–10% of portfolio as a hedge

REITs — Real Estate Investment Trusts

REITs let you invest in commercial real estate (office parks, malls, warehouses) through the stock exchange — without buying actual property. You earn rental income as dividends.

  • Listed on NSE/BSE — Brookfield India, Mindspace, Embassy Office Parks
  • Minimum investment: ~₹200–500 per unit (accessible)
  • Dividend yield: ~5–7% annually + capital appreciation
  • Good for income investors who want real estate exposure without crores of capital

P2P Lending

Platforms like Lendbox, Faircent connect borrowers and lenders directly. You can earn 10–16% returns by lending money to individuals. Risk: borrowers can default; unlike FDs, there's no guarantee. RBI-regulated but not insured.

Angel Investing / Startups

Investing in early-stage startups. Very high risk — most startups fail. But the 1-in-10 that succeed can return 50–100x. Only for experienced investors with high net worth, through platforms like LetsVenture or AngelList India.

The right mindset for alternative investments

  • Build your core portfolio first (equity MFs, PPF, health insurance, emergency fund)
  • Allocate only 5–15% of your portfolio to alternatives
  • Understand what you're buying — never invest in something you can't explain simply
  • Don't chase past returns — what went up 10x last year won't necessarily repeat
